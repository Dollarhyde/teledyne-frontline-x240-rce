# Disclaimer

This repository documents two vulnerabilities in the Teledyne LeCroy Frontline product line and provides proof-of-concept code that demonstrates them. It is published for defensive and educational purposes.

Coordinated disclosure. These issues were reported to Teledyne LeCroy on 2026-05-27. The vendor investigated, released patches, and published advisories TDY-PSG-2026-001 and TDY-PSG-2026-002 on 2026-06-24 and 2026-06-25, crediting the reporter. This material is released after patches became available and after a standard ninety-day window.
